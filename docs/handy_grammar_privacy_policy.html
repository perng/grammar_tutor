隱私權政策

最後更新日期： 2026 年 1 月 3 日

感謝您使用「單手學:英文文法」（以下簡稱「本應用程式」）。我們非常重視您的隱私權，本隱私權政策旨在說明我們如何處理您的資訊。

1. 資料收集與使用 (Data Collection)

本應用程式採用本地儲存優先的架構。這意味著：

  • 無個人資料收集： 我們不會收集您的姓名、電子郵件、電話號碼或任何可識別個人的資訊。

2. 兒童隱私 (Children’s Privacy & COPPA)

本應用程式設計適合所有年齡層的用戶，包括 13 歲以下的兒童。我們致力於遵守《兒童線上隱私權保護法》(COPPA)。我們不會有意收集 13 歲以下兒童的個人資訊。由於本應用程式不收集任何個人資料，也不使用任何追蹤用戶的第三方分析或廣告服務，因此兒童可以安全使用。如果家長或監護人發現其子女在未經同意的情況下向我們提供了個人資訊，請透過 happysaur.dev@gmail.com 聯繫我們，我們將刪除相關資訊。

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 3, 2026

Thank you for using "Handy Grammar" (hereinafter referred to as "this Application"). We value your privacy very much. This Privacy Policy aims to explain how we handle your information.

1. Data Collection and Use

This application adopts a "Local Storage First" architecture. This means:

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any personally identifiable information.
  • Learning Records: Your learning data, such as reading progress, quiz scores, bookmarks, and notes, are completely stored in the local database of your device (Shared Preferences / SQLite). We cannot access or view this data.
  • No External Tracking: This application does not use Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or other third-party user behavior tracking tools.

2. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

This application is designed for users of all ages, including children. We are committed to complying with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Since our app does not collect any personal data or use any third-party analytics/advertising services that track users, it is safe for children to use. If a parent or guardian becomes aware that their child has provided us with Personal Information without their consent, please contact us at happysaur.dev@gmail.com.

3. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We recommend that you review this page periodically for the latest information. Any changes will be effective immediately upon posting on this page.
